GOS Safety Guardrails (Panshet Dam)

• Gates operate only in symmetric pairs — never a single gate or three at once.
• Opening order: Gate 1 & 4 (outer pair) first, then Gate 2 & 3 (inner pair) — sequence 1‑4‑2‑3.
• Closing order reverses: 3‑2‑4‑1.
• No single continuous gate movement > 0.25 m; alternate outer/inner pairs in steps of ≤0.5 m until target opening or full clearance is reached.
• Do not let reservoir level encroach on freeboard: MWL 636.72 m, TBL 639.13 m. FRL is 636.27 m.
• Conservation storage should not be prematurely spilled during the tail end of September.
